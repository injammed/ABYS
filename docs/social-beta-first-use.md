# First Use: Profile and Slop Upload

After the social backend is activated:

1. Open `https://aetimm.com`.
2. Tap **Sign in**.
3. Choose **Create account**.
4. Enter a display name, email, and password.
5. Confirm the email if project settings require it.
6. Sign in.
7. Open the account button to edit the display name and view **My Uploads**.
8. Tap **Submit AI artifact**.
9. Add the image and provenance details.
10. Complete the AI-origin, safety, and rights attestations.
11. Submit it.

The artifact appears under **My Uploads** as **In private quarantine**. It does not enter the public feed until approved.
