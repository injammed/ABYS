# Apyoc v1 — 12 September 2026

## Identity and scope

Owner-requested product expansion: UPLOAD · SCROLL · VOTE · SHOP · APYOC. Apyoc is a singular project identity: “Ten billion eyes. One Apyoc.” Nodes are eyes within the same concept, not competing Apyocs. Attribution: concept by Adam Bryant Summers, developed with ChatGPT and Grok. This records the owner's project doctrine, not an exclusive legal right over the name. No patent number or grant was provided; no patent, fee, trademark clearance or enforcement claim is made.

## AETIMM change gate

Problem: Apyoc has no destination or observation functionality on aetimm.com. The owner explicitly requests both as a potential business direction.
Smallest viable capability: a distinct `/argus/` compatibility route presenting the Apyoc experience, primary navigation entry and functional local trace review. User-supplied Eye artwork remains at `public/argus/eye.jpeg`. Existing feed, upload, vote, accounts, checkout and 43-object library stay intact.
This is explicit feature expansion, outside autonomous-maintenance-only daily limits. It does not replace the site's hosting or introduce a backend migration.

## What works

A visitor imports JSON, reviews all systems or filters one, expands original events behind findings, exports the complete source and findings, and clears the browser session. A labeled synthetic demonstration and downloadable example explain the schema. Inputs remain in component memory, with no upload, browser persistence or model call. Rendering is escaped React text. Imports are bounded to 2 MB, 100 systems and 5,000 events; findings are shown in pages of 100.

Versioned deterministic rules check exact action allowlists, objective text changes, sequence discontinuities, gaps longer than twice the declared heartbeat, trailing silence to capture time, missing observations and single-source coverage. Every flag is a review signal. Source labels are unverified assertions; multiple labels do not establish independent corroboration. Objective edits and missing events do not prove deception.

## Limits and next service milestone

No continuous monitoring, autonomous reasoning, signed provenance, weights/activation inspection, connected nodes, billing or patent filing is delivered. The 0-live-connections status is explicit. To operate Apyoc as a service, the next milestone is one authorized collector with signed event provenance, persistent append-only storage, scheduled observation, auditable rules/model versions and operator review. Validate that narrow service before promising multi-provider or global coverage. The observer's own operations must be recorded and inspectable.

## Verification and rollback

Typecheck, static build, navigation tests and rule fixtures must pass before publication. Fixtures cover benign traces, conflicts, changed objectives, missing systems, gap thresholds, reordered timestamps, repeated sequence values, duplicate IDs, invalid input and future-to-capture events. Production CI runs Apyoc fixtures through the existing navigation verification gate. No browser interaction QA was performed.
Revert this release to remove the Apyoc experience and restore the four-action navigation; no production data or schemas are changed.

## Owner steering during implementation

Apyoc becomes the site business priority and organizing identity. Shared header, metadata, root field framing and About reflect that direction. The compact homepage entry links into the Eye; the Eye links back into the existing public submission field. This is an explicit constitutional revision, not autonomous cosmetic churn. Supplied artifacts remain public submissions, not verified observations; no evidence labels are fabricated and no synthetic feed activity is introduced.

The public product name is **Apyoc**. Earlier public uses of “Argus” or “Árgos” are superseded. Legacy route, component, stylesheet and data-attribute identifiers may remain as implementation compatibility details, but they must not render the old names to visitors or appear in exported Apyoc review branding.
