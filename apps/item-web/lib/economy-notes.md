# Cash, equipment and Uk’onu’okele

This is browser-local fictional USD, separate from the library shop and any real payment system. New players have $250. Hostile defeats award cash and score by archetype; rescues pay $500 and 5,000 points per full convoy. The score is cumulative and cash is spendable. Purchases, equipment, skull progress and uncollected souls are saved with the existing player, capital and Horizon campaign states.

34 vending machines have physical models, map markers and accessible use positions checked against the DC building footprints. The catalog includes ammunition (up to 999 reserve), water, food, medicine, armor refill, helmet, exo suit, repulsor jetpack and nanites. Duplicate permanent upgrades and invalid purchases never debit cash. Armor absorbs 70% of incoming damage while available; helmet reduces damage by 15%; exo improves movement/sprint endurance. Jetpack flight consumes fuel, respects buildings/bunker roof, and recharges only when grounded. Nanites heal after eight seconds without combat damage and never revive a dead player. Flight saves resume on safe cathedral ground while preserving inventory and wallet.

Mara starts the skull quest. Protect 5,000 people and defeat three enemies, then return to claim it. Key 3 equips the skull, revealing nearby ghostly souls. Click/F absorbs ten individual souls per second within 18m and only with an unobstructed path. Each local defeated entity leaves one soul, including alien, robot, demon and wizard representatives. Regional zombie clearance, demon banishment and civilian deaths are reconciled against cumulative campaign counts; they do not duplicate local kill souls. No cash/kill score is awarded for abstract regional clearance.

The pending ledger is bounded to 512 spatial cohorts. A cohort can hold multiple individually counted souls; merging cohorts at capacity preserves the total rather than deleting souls. Rendering uses 48 reusable visible spirits. The invariant is departed = pending + held + sold. The wallet invariant is cash = 250 + earned + sold × 50 − spent. Legacy saves gain the new state without resetting the existing campaigns; known prior regional deaths and defeated robot/alien IDs are reconciled once.

Nera buys held souls for $50 each in the tunnel at (170, 500), reached by a six-meter descent. The aerial surface has an actual opening, a ramp matching the movement elevation, walls, ceiling, lighting and protected NPC. Ground/ceiling ray tests and footprint reachability checks run with the economics tests through verify-survival.mjs. WebGL-capable browser playtesting remains necessary for visual/flight polish.
